## How Anjana Works With AI

This is the throughline for AI-focused roles, so it sits up front.

**She builds AI products, not just AI-flavored features.** At Gabriel AI she designed and shipped 4 MCP servers in Python and TypeScript that expose 28 tool-calling functions across a Django backend, Firebase Realtime Database, and Stripe billing. The point of those servers is to let internal agents and teammates query backend, billing, and realtime data through one standard interface instead of waiting on an engineer to run a pull. That is the same agent-plus-tool-calling pattern behind most consumer AI assistants shipping today.

**She understands the AI/ML concepts that matter for product work.** Model inference and its tradeoffs, API and tool-calling design, MCP servers and the Tools, Resources, and Prompts they expose, agentic task execution, and RAG-style retrieval. She speaks to these as a builder, not from a slide.

**She instruments AI features on the metrics that count.** On Gabriel's multi-tenant voice pipeline (AWS Lambda, ElevenLabs, Telnyx, Redis, Firebase) she tracks usage, latency, and adoption across pilot accounts, then prioritizes and cuts features against business impact.

**She uses AI tooling to accelerate her own work.** A Claude Code agent workflow and team assistants cut engineering delivery from 5 days to 2. Gemini CLI compressed competitive and requirement research at Trading Technologies. LLM-driven automations cut manual coordination effort by 60% at Northeastern. This is the "use AI to multiply your impact and accelerate POCs" skill, already in practice.

**She treats AI responsibly.** She is mindful of performance (latency budgets on the voice pipeline), data handling (customer and billing data flowing through Stripe and Firebase), and real user impact when deciding what ships.

**Honest scope note for recruiters:** Anjana's AI work has been cloud and SaaS, not native on-device or embedded. The architecture transfers cleanly to on-device and edge contexts, since agents, tool-calling, and inference instrumentation are the same regardless of where the model runs, and she ramps fast on platform specifics like Android or Windows when a role needs it.
